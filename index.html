About

Empirical economics, real-world questions

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Newcastle University Business School (since September 2024), supervised by Nils Braakmann and Harry Pickard. My research applies microeconometric methods to questions in migration, crime, and labour economics. I am based in Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom, and work in Turkish (native), English, and German.
