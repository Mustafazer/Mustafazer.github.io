About

Empirical economics, real-world questions

I am a PhD student in Economics at Newcastle University Business School, supervised by Nils Braakmann and Harry Pickard. My research applies microeconometric methods to questions in migration, crime, and labour economics. I am based in Newcastle upon Tyne, United Kingdom.
